Durian AI is a computer-vision quality assessment platform designed for the global durian industry.

Rather than providing a simple ripe/unripe classification, Durian AI evaluates multiple quality dimensions including ripeness, freshness, texture, and overall quality grade. The platform aims to help consumers make informed purchasing decisions while enabling retailers, exporters, and growers to standardize quality assessment at scale.

The current prototype demonstrates an end-to-end AI workflow, including image upload, automated analysis, quality reporting, historical tracking, and explainability visualization. Future development will focus on training proprietary computer-vision models, batch inspection workflows, and enterprise analytics for large-scale agricultural operations.
